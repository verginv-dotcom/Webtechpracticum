About Our System

What is this System?

This system analyzes student marks from IA-1, IA-2 and IA-3. It identifies strong subjects, weak subjects and performance improvements.

Main Features

  • Analyze internal assessment marks
  • Identify strong and weak subjects
  • Track academic performance
  • Provide study suggestions
  • Help students prepare for upcoming exams

Academic Guidance

Study Improvement Suggestions

Students can improve their academic performance by following a proper study plan and focusing on subjects that need more attention.

  1. Identify weak subjects.
  2. Prepare a daily study timetable.
  3. Practice important questions.
  4. Revise previous lessons regularly.
  5. Prepare well for upcoming assessments.
