About Our System

What is this System?

This smart system analyzes student marks from IA-1, IA-2 and IA-3. It identifies strong subjects, weak subjects and academic improvements.

Main Features

  • Analyze internal assessment marks.
  • Identify strong and weak subjects.
  • Track overall academic performance.
  • Provide useful study suggestions.
  • Help students prepare for upcoming exams.

  3. Practice important questions.
  4. Revise previous lessons regularly.
  5. Prepare well for upcoming assessments.
